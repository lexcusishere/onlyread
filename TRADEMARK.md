# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **OnlyRead** name, the
OnlyRead logo, the monogram icon and the wordmark (the "marks") are not covered by that license. They
identify the official OnlyRead extension and OnlyRead Pro, so that users know what they are
installing and whom they are trusting with their browser.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your project is "based on OnlyRead", "a fork of OnlyRead" or "compatible with OnlyRead".
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and academic work about the project.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official builds, keeping the name and icons.
- Use the marks in your own local or internal builds that you do not distribute.

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish a modified build, fork or derivative (in any browser store, website or package registry)
  under the OnlyRead name, logo, icons or a confusingly similar name, icon or domain.
- Suggest that your product, service or company is made, endorsed or supported by OnlyRead AI (In Formation).
- Use the marks in a product name, company name, domain name or social media handle.
- Offer a hosted service called OnlyRead, or one that presents itself as OnlyRead Pro.

## If you fork

Rename it, and replace the icons in `extension/assets/` and the wordmark in the UI. Keep the license
and copyright notices, as the AGPL requires. A line such as "based on OnlyRead" in your description is
welcome.

## Questions

Open an issue at https://github.com/onlyreadai/onlyread/issues. When in doubt, ask. We are happy to
say yes to community uses.
